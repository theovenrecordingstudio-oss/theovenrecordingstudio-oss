# David Click

**Audio engineer · Founder of The Oven · Audio-tool creator**

I’m David, an audio engineer and independent audio-tool creator based near Austin, Texas.

I spent ten years running The Oven recording studio, working with artists, mentoring engineers, and helping people turn ideas into finished recordings.

Today, I’m bringing that studio experience into audio software:

- **CRUSH:** A released AU/VST3 bitcrusher.
- **MILK:** A resonance-control plugin in development, designed to smooth harshness in vocals and other audio.
- **Audio education:** Plugin demonstrations and tutorials, including narration created with a custom AI voice based on my own recordings.

My focus is practical: useful tools, clear explanations, and good sound.
